01 / THE STUDIO

Small studio.
Built for the bigger picture.

144p Group is a small, hands-on creative studio specializing in graphic design, digital marketing assets, print design, and production-ready artwork.

We help marketing agencies, eCommerce brands, retailers, and growing businesses create consistent, high-quality visual materials without the complexity of managing multiple designers.

Our team combines extensive experience in retail design, campaign production, editorial layouts, packaging, and professional print preparation. From digital advertising creatives to multi-page catalogs and technically accurate production files, we handle both the creative and practical sides of design.
