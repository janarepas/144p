05 / FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

A few things before we start.

How much does graphic design cost?

Pricing depends on the scope, the number of variations, and the deadline. Tell us what you need, and we’ll put together a tailored quote once we’ve agreed on the details.

What should I include in my brief?

Describe what you need, where it will be used, and your deadline. Share any brand guidelines, copy, images, or references you already have.

Can you provide ongoing creative support?

Yes. We can support your team with campaign variations, regular creatives, and publication updates. We agree on the tasks, schedule, and available capacity in advance.

What files will I receive?

We deliver the agreed formats for your intended use, such as digital images, print-ready PDFs, or vector artwork. Editable source files are also agreed as part of the quote.
